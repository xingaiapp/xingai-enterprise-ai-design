# Claim Business Course

Complete bilingual training for IT Solution Architects and Software Engineers who need to design claims systems (Property / CTABS-style programs, Sedgwick-style TPA engagements) without prior insurance expertise.

## Audience

- Experienced architects / engineers (Azure, .NET, APIs, event-driven design)
- New to claims terminology, adjuster operations, reserves, payments, TPA models
- May join Sedgwick-style Property / CTABS claim system projects

## Accuracy posture

Guides teach **general industry practice**, **typical TPA operating models**, and **illustrative architecture**. They do **not** document confidential Sedgwick processes, schemas, internal APIs, or client-specific rules. Actual workflows depend on Client Instructions, Service Agreements, Policy terms, jurisdiction, and Delegated Authority. Sedgwick is generally a **TPA / claims administration partner**, not necessarily the Insurance Carrier.

## How to study

1. Read EN or ZH opening + one-page mental model.
2. Complete Levels 1–2 before Architecture chapters.
3. Work the Property case end-to-end with pencil math for Reserve / Payment.
4. Use Level 5–6 + workshops before writing ADRs or solution outlines.
5. Pass the 30-question assessment (≥80% recommended gate).
